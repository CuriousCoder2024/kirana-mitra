Live targets: API `https://kirana-mitra-api-396621755403.asia-south1.run.app` (revision `kirana-mitra-api-00005` at the
start, `00006` with `STORE=firestore` from 23:07 IST; `/health` reported `gemini-3.8-flash` throughout) and the web build at
`https://storage.googleapis.com/kirana-mitra-app-web/index.html`. `https://kirana-mitra-app.web.app` returned 404 for the
whole run, so only the GCS build was tested. Method: a stdlib Python harness (every request and response logged to JSONL,

that says "run 1" was on the in-memory revision; "run 2" on Firestore. Behaviour was identical apart from the restart.

## Part A findings, ranked

### P1

**A1. A repayment from an unknown customer creates a negative balance.** Tag: backend.
Repro: new shop, `POST /chat` "Sharma ji ne 200 wapas kiye" (reproduced twice, run 1 and run 2).
Observed: `udhaar_paid`, applied, `reply_text` = `Samjha: Sharma ji ne ₹200 wapas kiye. Total ₹-200.`; `/ledger`
shows `Sharma ji: -200` and `total_outstanding` ignores it (1050 when the signed sum was 850).
Expected: no ledger write, or a question. Suggested reply: `Sharma ji ka koi udhaar nahi likha hai. Kya ₹200 advance
likhoon?` with a confirm chip.
Fix: in the service, refuse `udhaar_paid` that would take a balance below 0 (hold with `applied:false`), and decide
whether `total_outstanding` is the signed sum or positive-only; today it is positive-only and undocumented.

**A2. Gemini tail latency reaches 75 to 78 s and then fails.** Tag: backend / infra.
Repro: any endpoint under light load (one request at a time plus one browser user). Observed: "1 colgate bika" took
75.8 s; the first `/voice-note` (`v1.m4a`, "कल पारले जी के तीन कार्टन आए") and the first real-photo `/shelf-scan`
(`docs/design/assets/photos/shelves/shelf_01.jpg`) both returned `502 {"code":"gemini_failed","message":"ServerError:
504 DEADLINE_EXCEEDED ..."}` after about 78 s. Retrying the identical clip later took 3.8 s. 14 of 59 chats were over
10 s. The app's receive timeout is 60 s, so the user sees the generic error bubble before the server even gives up.
Expected: the contract's 40 s cap plus one retry; a reply within what the app waits for.
Fix: set the per-call deadline to 25 s with one retry (total under 60 s), and make the error message a shopkeeper
sentence (`Server thoda slow hai, dobara bolo.`) instead of the raw Vertex text.

**A3. A real shelf photo yields a reply made of seven alternative sentences joined with " / ".** Tag: prompt / backend.
Repro: `POST /shelf-scan` with `docs/design/assets/photos/shelves/shelf_03.jpg` (a real Wikimedia shop shelf).
Observed (200 in 4.4 s, `items: []`): `Shelf pe catalogue ka koi bhi product clearly identify nahi ho raha hai. Sabhi
items unbranded sacks, sachets ya alag brands ke hain. Shelf pe 0 catalogue items dikhe. / Catalogue se koi item match
nahi hua. Shelf pe dikha: kuch nahi. Ek baar shelf ki clear aur close photo bhejiye! / Catalogue me se koi product shelf
pe clearly nahi dikh raha hai. / Shelf pe koi catalogue item clearly visible nahi hai. / Shelf pe catalogue ka koi
product nahi dikha. / Shelf pe catalogue item: 0. / Shelf pe catalogue ka koi item nahi dikha.`
Expected: one sentence. Suggested: `Shelf pe catalogue ka koi item nahi dikha. Thoda paas se, seedhi photo bhejo.`
Fix: when `items` is empty, drop the model text and use a fixed reply; also cap `reply_text` at two sentences before
appending the "kam hai" list. The two eval images counted perfectly (shelf_01_easy 6/12/3 exact; shelf_03_crowded
11/5/13/4/6/3/4 against labels 11/5/14/4/6/3/4, one Parle-G off), so vision is fine; it is the empty case that breaks.

**A4. Voice replies to a Hinglish shop come back in Devanagari.** Tag: prompt.
Repro: shop language `hi-Latn`, `POST /voice-note` with any Hindi/Hinglish clip. Observed: transcript is written in
Devanagari, `language: "hi"`, and the reply follows the transcript script: `समझा: गुप्ता जी, ₹350 उधार। कुल बकाया ₹350.`
(3 of 3 clips to the Hinglish shop). Worse, one reply mixed both: `Samjha: Maggi ke 10 packet aur Colgate ke 2 piece
bikri darj ki gayi. अब स्टॉक: Maggi 12 packet, Colgate 12 piece.` (Gemini wrote Roman, the backend suffix came out
Devanagari because `language` said `hi`).
Expected: spoken Hindi has no script, so the reply should use the shop's script; the owner reads Hinglish.
Fix: for `/voice-note`, force `language` to the shop language (keep the transcript in whatever script Gemini chose), and
build the backend suffix from the shop language, not from the detected one.

### P2

**A5. Udhaar questions are never answered.** Tag: backend / prompt.
Repro: "Gupta ji ka kitna udhaar hai?" (run 1, balance 350) and "Verma ji ka kitna udhaar hai?" (run 2, balance 100).
Observed: `question`, 0.95, `Gupta ji ka udhaar khata check kar raha hoon.` and `Verma ji ka udhaar khata check karke
batata hoon.` Nothing follows. Stock questions work (`Parle-G 70g abhi 104 packet bacha hai.`), so the backend injects
stock but not the ledger. Suggested reply: `Gupta ji ka udhaar ₹350 baaki hai.`
Fix: answer `question` with `customer_name` from the ledger in the service, the way stock questions are answered.

**A6. "khatam ho gaya" is acknowledged as noted but nothing is written.** Tag: prompt / backend.
Repro: "nescafe khatam ho gaya" (twice). Observed: `unclear`, confidence 0.7, `applied:false`, no chips, reply
`Samjha: Nescafe stock khatam note kar liya.` / `Samjha: Nescafe khatam ho gaya note kiya.` Stock stays 6. The word
"Samjha" is the app's signal that something was written.
Expected: `Nescafe ab 0 kar doon? Abhi 6 piece likha hai.` with a confirm chip that sets the quantity to 0.
Fix: teach the prompt a "set to zero" case, and never let an `unclear` reply start with "Samjha".

**A7. Confirming a held entry replays the question as the confirmation.** Tag: backend.
Repro: "shayad 5 maggi aaye, ya 6" → run 2 gave `stock_in` 0.55, held, reply `Kitne packet aaye the - 5 ya 6? Sahi
hai?`, chip "Haan, add karo". `POST /confirm` wrote 5 and replied `Kitne packet aaye the - 5 ya 6? Ab total: Maggi 35
packet.` The shopkeeper confirmed and is asked the question again, with the 5 never stated.
Expected: `Samjha: Maggi, 5 packet stock mein add kiya. Ab total: Maggi 35 packet.`
Fix: on confirm, rebuild the first sentence from the entities (template) instead of reusing the held reply. Also note the
same sentence came back as `unclear` with no chips in run 1 and in the web app (see B4), so the demo's "Pakka nahi" card
is a coin flip.

**A8. Hindi replies carry Roman units and English words.** Tag: backend.
Repro: shop language `hi`, any stock entry. Observed in 6 of 6: `समझा: Parle-G, 2 कार्टन स्टॉक में जोड़े गए। अब स्टॉक:
Parle-G 90 packet (2 carton = 48 packet).` Gemini writes कार्टन; the backend suffix writes `packet`, `carton`, `piece`.
Expected: `अब स्टॉक: Parle-G 90 पैकेट (2 कार्टन = 48 पैकेट).` Brand names in Roman are fine.
Fix: localise the unit words in the suffix per shop language (packet/पैकेट, carton/कार्टन, piece/पीस, kg/किलो, litre/लीटर).

**A9. "do kilo tata salt aaya" writes 2 packets and says both.** Tag: prompt.
Observed: `Samjha: Tata Salt, 2 kg stock mein add kiya. Ab total: Tata Salt 22 packet.` Tata Salt is a 1 kg packet, so
the number happens to be right, but "2 kg" then "22 packet" reads as two different things, and for Aashirvaad 5 kg the
same path would write 5 packets for "5 kilo". Suggested: `Samjha: Tata Salt 1kg, 2 packet add kiya. Ab total 22 packet.`
Fix: convert kg/litre to packs using `pack_size` before writing, and reply in the stock unit.

**A10. Summary narration leaks English and misdates past days.** Tag: prompt.
Observed today: `... Low-stock items mein abhi none hai, baki sab theek raha.` Observed for `?date=2026-10-10`:
`Aaj QA Hinglish Store par koi sale nahi hui aur 0 entries record ki gayi hain. ... kal ka din behtar rahega.` A past
date is narrated as "Aaj" and ends with a pep line. Both are three sentences long where the fixed template is one.
Suggested: `10 Oct ka hisaab: bikri ₹0, 0 entries. Udhaar baaki ₹100. Koi item kam nahi.`
Fix: pass the date word ("Aaj" / "Kal" / "10 Oct") into the prompt, forbid English fillers, and cap at two sentences.
The numbers themselves (`sales_total`, `entries`, `udhaar_*`) were correct on every call and undone entries were excluded.

**A11. `/languages` does not match the contract.** Tag: docs / backend.
Contract: `name_en`, `name_native`. Live: `name`, `native_name`. Fix one of them.

**A12. Validation errors are Python reprs.** Tag: backend.
Every 400 returns `"message": "[{'type': 'missing', 'loc': ('body', 'text'), ...}]"`. The contract says "human
readable". The custom ones (`audio too large (max 10 MB / 60 s)`, `unsupported image type ...`, `date must be
YYYY-MM-DD`) are good. Fix: map pydantic errors to `"text is required"` style strings.

**A13. No low-stock nudge after a sale.** Tag: backend.
"twenty packets of magi sold" left Maggi at 5 (reorder 12) and replied `Got it: Recorded sale of 20 packets of Maggi.
Now in stock: Maggi 5 packet.` The shelf-scan path already adds "kam hai (reorder 12)"; the sale path does not.
Suggested: `... Ab total: Maggi 5 packet. Kam hai, reorder 12.`

### P3

- **A15** `Total ₹1,200` vs `Total ₹350`: comma grouping only above 999, and Indian grouping would be `₹1,200` anyway;

- **A16** "asdfgh qwerty" is tagged `language: "en"` while the reply is Hinglish (prompt). Harmless.

- **A18** `POST /reorder` `lines[].rank/cover_days/demand_ratio` are `null` for manual orders; the contract shows values
  (docs).
- **A19** The `(3 carton = 72 packet)` tail is helpful once and noise afterwards; consider dropping it when the user

- **A20** "nahi, 5 nahi 6" with no context gives `Kaunsa item ya udhaar 6 karna hai? Thoda detail batao.` That is a fair

  strong demo moment (backend, later).

### What worked (API)

- Every write was reflected exactly in `/stock` and `/ledger` immediately after; running totals in `reply_text` matched
  the store on all 43 applied entries, including carton to packet (24 per carton) and two items in one sentence.
- Undo: single undo restores the previous quantity or balance; a second undo of the same message gives 409
  `undo_expired`; undo of a message older than the last 10 applied gives 409; undo of a confirmed shelf scan restores all
  three quantities (`Theek hai, hata diya. Maggi 17 packet; Parle-G 86 packet; Tata Salt 23 packet.`). Confirming the
  same scan twice gives 404 `No pending message with that id`.
- Error shapes: bad shop id → 404 `not_found` on chat, stock, ledger, forecast, summary; text/plain to `/voice-note` → 400;
  an audio file to `/shelf-scan` → 400; a 134 s, 11 MB WAV → 400 `audio too large (max 10 MB / 60 s)` in 0.7 s; JSON to
  a multipart endpoint → 400; bad language on `/shops` → 400 listing the 24 codes; `/auto-restock/run` without the
  token → 401. No 500s anywhere. `/healthz` is 404 at the edge as the contract says.
- Forecast, top-items, reorder, orders, messages and PATCH all answered in under 0.2 s with the contract shapes; Dussehra
  (20 Oct) and Diwali (8 Nov) are in `festivals`; `whatsapp_url` picks up `distributor_phone` after the PATCH.
- Hinglish typed into the English shop was answered in Hinglish, as the contract's "mirror the user's mix" rule says.
- Numbers as words ("das", "teen", "दो सौ", "five", "twenty", "ek dozen" → 12) were all read correctly; "parle ji" and
  "magi" matched the catalogue; "5 kilo chawal aaya" created a new item `Chawal 5 kg` with a sensible reply.
- Good replies to copy from: `Samjha: Gupta ji, ₹350 udhaar. Total ₹350.`, `Samjha: Maggi, 5 packet bike. Ab total:
  Maggi 25 packet.`, `Got it: Mehta paid back ₹100. Balance ₹200.`, `Main sirf dukan ke hisab-kitab aur stock mein madad
  kar sakta hoon. Kuch entry karni hai?`

## Part B findings (web app, GCS build)

**B1. A stale shop id is shown to the shopkeeper as a raw error, and the app does not re-register.** Tag: app. P1.
Repro: with a working shop, set `localStorage["flutter.shop_id"]` to an id the server no longer has, reload, type
"3 maggi bike". Observed: welcome bubble, then a red bubble `shop shp_00E59D21B08E97ED not found` (screenshot 13). The
id stays in storage; every further message fails the same way. The only way out is the three-dot menu → "दुकान बदलें",
which returns to an empty onboarding form (screenshot 14). This is exactly what a Cloud Run restart does to every
existing user, and it happened once during this run.
Fix: on a 404 `not_found` from `/chat`, `/stock` or `/ledger`, call `POST /shops` with the stored name, owner and
language, swap the id, retry the request once, and show `Dukaan dobara register ki, chaliye.` at most.

**B2. Stock entries get a plain bubble, udhaar entries get the Samjha card.** Tag: app. P2.
Repro: type "Gupta ji ne 350 rupaye udhaar liya" then "Kal Parle-G ke 3 carton aaye". Observed: the first shows the
green "Samjha · likh diya" card with `+ ₹350` and `Udhaar · ab total ₹350`; the second is a text bubble with the full
`Samjha: Parle-G, 3 carton stock mein add kiya. Ab total: Parle-G 114 packet (3 carton = 72 packet).` and chips
beneath (screenshots 02 and 04). The demo script predicts this. Fix: parse `Ab total: <name> <n> <unit>` in
`_parseTotal`, or better, have the backend return `new_total` as a field (the design hand-over asked for this).

**B3. The language toggle changes the chrome only.** Tag: app / backend. P2.
Repro: switch the app-bar chip to हिंदी, type "2 maggi bike". Observed: tabs, dock and card labels switch to Hindi, the
Undo chip becomes "अनडू", but the reply is `Samjha: Maggi, 2 packet bik gaye. Ab total: Maggi 28 packet.` and the
server chip stays "Stock dekho" (screenshot 09). The shop's `language` on the server is unchanged (and `PATCH /shops`
does not accept `language`). Fix: send `language` in `PATCH /shops` on toggle, and translate "Undo" properly
("वापस लो") rather than transliterating.

**B4. Ambiguous entry shows no "Pakka nahi" card.** Tag: prompt / app. P2.
Repro: "shayad 5 maggi aaye, ya 6". Observed: plain bubble `Maggi ke kitne packet aaye, 5 ya 6?` with no Haan / Nahi
buttons (screenshot 08), because the API returned `unclear` with no chips this time (see A7 for the run where it
returned `stock_in` 0.55 with a confirm chip). The reply itself is a good question. Fix: make the prompt return
`stock_in` with low confidence whenever an item and a candidate quantity are present, so the chip appears.

**B5. Hisaab card says "Dussehra 1 हफ्ते दूर है" on 18 Oct for a festival on 20 Oct.** Tag: app. P3.
Also the summary line under the tiles rounds to weeks. Suggest days when under 7 (screenshot 11).

**B6. Onboarding label overlap when typing by keyboard.** Tag: app. P3.
With the pane's synthetic keyboard the "Dukaan ka naam" hint stayed drawn over the typed text (the floating label did
not animate). It rendered correctly when the field was filled through the DOM. Likely a Flutter-web focus quirk; worth a
real-device check only.

**B7. Forecast mic FAB overlaps the reason band.** Tag: app. P3.
At 375 px the floating mic covers the word "packet" in `Stock mein 18 · mangao 264 packet` (screenshot 06).

**B8. After "दुकान बदलें" the onboarding form is empty.** Tag: app. P3.
Shop and owner name are cleared, so a restart costs the owner retyping both (screenshot 14). Pre-fill them.

### What worked (web)

- Onboarding in Hinglish (screenshot 01), `POST /shops` succeeded and the chat opened with the welcome bubble.
- Udhaar Samjha card, Undo greys the card and adds `Theek hai, hata diya. Gupta ji ₹350 → Total ₹0.` (screenshots 02, 03).
- Stock tab lists 15 items with reorder levels and the "Sab theek hai" band; Udhaar tab shows the empty state after undo

- Forecast tab: chart with history, forecast, band, Dussehra and Diwali markers, reason band, "Order banao · 264 packet";

- Likho text box, send, typing state; language toggle sheet; hisaab card from the menu with correct numbers (₹28 for 2

- Hold-to-talk: the pane blocks the microphone, and the app showed `वॉइस नोट के लिए माइक की अनुमति चाहिए।` (screenshot 10).
  Not testable further here; voice was covered at the API level.
- Not tested: the shelf-photo button (the pane cannot drive a file picker from a remote origin, and the new build it was

## Top 5 to fix before judging

1. **Re-register silently on a 404 shop (B1, app).** One Cloud Run restart during the judges' window turns every
   installed app into a red "shop not found" bubble.
2. **Cap Gemini at 25 s with one retry and give the error a Hinglish sentence (A2, backend).** Three calls out of 68
   sat past 60 s; the app gives up at 60 s and shows "Couldn't reach the server".
3. **Reply script follows the shop, not the transcript (A4 and A8, prompt and backend).** Hinglish owners get Devanagari
   voice replies, Hindi owners get `packet`/`carton` in their Devanagari, and one reply had both scripts in one line.
4. **Three replies that say one thing and do another (A1, A5, A6, backend and prompt).** `Total ₹-200`, "check kar
   raha hoon" that never answers, and "Samjha ... note kiya" that writes nothing. These are the replies the owner
   means by "not clear".
5. **Fixed one-liners where the model rambles (A3, A7, A10, backend).** The empty shelf scan, the confirm echo and the
   summary narration should all come from templates, not from Gemini.
